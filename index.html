FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before connecting your account.

What exactly is MOPKO?

MOPKO is an analytics and coaching app for short-video creators. It combines your own performance data with AI analysis to tell you what to improve, when to post, and what to work on next.

Does MOPKO post on my behalf?

No. MOPKO only reads data that you authorize. It never publishes, edits, deletes or sends anything on your behalf.

Which platforms are supported?

MOPKO works with TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Snapchat Spotlight. Support for additional platforms is added regularly during early access.

How does the AI draft analysis work?

You upload a draft video and MOPKO reviews its hook, pacing, subtitles, structure and clarity. You get concrete, actionable suggestions you can apply before publishing - not a vague score.

Can I revoke access to my accounts?

Yes, at any time. You can disconnect a platform from your settings in one click, and the associated data is deleted according to our Privacy Policy.

Is MOPKO free?

MOPKO is currently in early access. Join the waitlist to be notified when accounts open and to receive founding-member pricing.
