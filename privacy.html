Privacy Policy

MOPKO ("we", "the app") is an analytics and coaching tool for short-video creators. This policy explains what data we handle, why we handle it, and how you stay in control.

MOPKO only reads data that you explicitly authorize, through official platform APIs. We never post, comment, like or follow on your behalf, we never use your data for advertising, and we never sell it.

01 Data we access

When you choose to connect your TikTok account, and only with your consent, we access the following through TikTok's official APIs:

  • Basic profile information - unique account identifier, display name, username and avatar.
  • Account statistics - follower, following, likes and video counts.
  • Your video list and public metrics - title, cover, duration, views, likes, comments and shares.

You may also upload files yourself: statistics exports from TikTok Studio, draft videos for analysis, and comments you wish to analyze.

02 How we use it

  • To display your performance and its evolution over time.
  • To generate AI-based analyses, recommendations, daily missions and reports for your account.
  • To improve the app - for example, by analyzing the feedback you send us about MOPKO.

We do not post, comment, like or follow on your behalf. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

03 AI processing

To produce suggestions, we send summarized data - statistics, transcripts or key elements of a draft video - to an AI service provider. We send only what is needed for the requested analysis.

04 Storage and security

Data is stored on a managed cloud database. Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored server-side only and are never exposed to the app or to other users. Each user can only access their own data.

05 Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time in the app, or revoke MOPKO's access from your TikTok settings.

To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below and we will process the request within a reasonable time.

06 Sharing

We share data only with the infrastructure and AI providers needed to operate the service, or when the law requires it.

07 Children

MOPKO is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

08 Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
